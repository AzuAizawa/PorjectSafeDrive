Platform Settings

Every business rule lives here — nothing is hardcoded in the app.

Commission %
Added on top of base price for the renter; the platform keeps this.
Downpayment %
Share of total price due upfront to lock in a booking.
Cancellation fee %
Charged on total price if cancelled after the free-cancel window.
Free-cancel window (hrs)
Hours after downpayment where cancellation is still free.
Max deposit %
Cap on owner-set security deposits, as % of total price.
Free vehicle slots
Listings included before a subscription is required.
Subscription price (₱)
Monthly price for the extra-slots subscription.
Subscription slots
Extra listing slots granted per active subscription.
Owner response window (hrs)
Time an owner has to accept or decline a booking request.
Payment deadline (hrs)
Time a renter has to pay the downpayment after acceptance.
Auto-complete grace period (days)
Days after the end date before an undisputed rental auto-completes.
Strike threshold
Strikes before an account is auto-flagged for admin review.
